Privacy policy

GameLibrary Saves is a personal tool operated by and for a single person (the owner of this site).

What data it accesses

Only files that GameLibrary Saves itself creates in the owner's Google Drive (Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). These are backup copies of the owner's own video-game save files. It cannot see any other files in Google Drive.

How the data is used and stored

The files are uploaded from, and downloaded to, the owner's own computers so saves can be restored. They are stored only in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's computers.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Removing data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the files can be deleted from Google Drive.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in (consent) screen for this app.
